## The Permitting Process for Asphalt Paving Projects
The permitting process for asphalt paving projects in San Diego County involves several steps:
* Determine the type of permit required: Depending on the scope of your project, you may need a grading permit, street opening permit, or sidewalk construction permit.
* Submit the necessary documentation: This includes plans, specifications, and other supporting documents that detail the scope of work, materials, and timeline.
* Pay the required fees: Permit fees vary depending on the type of permit and the scope of the project.
* Schedule an inspection: Once the permit is issued, you'll need to schedule an inspection to ensure that the work is completed in accordance with the approved plans and specifications.
Some common permits required for asphalt paving projects in San Diego County include:
1. Grading permit: Required for projects that involve adding or replacing 750 square feet or more of paving.
2. Street opening permit: Required for any excavation or opening of a street, sidewalk, or public place.
3. Sidewalk construction permit: Needed for constructing or repairing sidewalks using approved materials.

## Compliance and Maintenance Tips
To ensure compliance and extend the lifespan of your asphalt paving project, it's essential to follow these maintenance tips:
* Regularly inspect the pavement for cracks, potholes, and other signs of wear and tear.
* Sealcoat the pavement every 2-3 years to protect it from the elements and prevent damage.
* Keep the pavement clean and clear of debris to prevent damage and maintain drainage.
* Consider implementing a preventative maintenance program to catch potential issues before they become major problems.
