## The Permitting Process for Asphalt Paving Projects
The permitting process for asphalt paving projects in San Diego County can vary depending on the scope and complexity of the project. However, here are some general steps to expect:
* Determine if a permit is required: Check with the City of San Diego's Development Services Department to determine if a permit is required for your project. For example, if you are planning to resurface an existing driveway in North Park, you may not need a permit, but if you are expanding the driveway or changing its configuration, you may need to obtain a permit.
* Submit a site plan: A site plan is required for most asphalt paving projects, including driveway paving and parking lot paving. The site plan should include simple drainage arrows and surface notes, such as asphalt, concrete, or pavers.
* Obtain approval: Once your site plan is submitted, it will be reviewed by the City of San Diego's Development Services Department to ensure compliance with local regulations and ordinances.
* Obtain the necessary permits: If your project requires a permit, you will need to obtain the necessary permits before commencing work.

Some common permits required for asphalt paving projects in San Diego include:
* Grading permit: Required for projects that add or replace 750 square feet or more of paving
* Driveway permit: Required for new driveway installations or modifications to existing driveways
* Stormwater permit: Required for projects that impact stormwater drainage, such as parking lot paving or asphalt resurfacing
