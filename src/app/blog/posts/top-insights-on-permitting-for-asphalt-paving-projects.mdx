## Understanding Permit Requirements for Asphalt Paving

Before starting your asphalt paving project, it's essential to determine if you need a permit. In San Diego, a grading permit is typically required if you add or replace 750 square feet or more of paving. However, if you're resurfacing existing paving, you may not need a permit. It's crucial to note that permit requirements can vary depending on the scope of your project, location, and local regulations. For instance, if you're planning to change the size, shape, or material of your driveway, you may need to obtain a permit due to potential impacts on drainage, property boundaries, and zoning regulations.
