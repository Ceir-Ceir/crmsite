## The Importance of Proper Debris Disposal

Proper renovation debris disposal is crucial in preserving our environment and maintaining a clean, safe job site. Disposing of material such as wood, concrete, metal, and plastic improperly can lead to pollution and potential harm to wildlife. By knowing what kind of waste you generate during renovations, you can make informed disposal decisions that are eco-friendly and cost-effective.
