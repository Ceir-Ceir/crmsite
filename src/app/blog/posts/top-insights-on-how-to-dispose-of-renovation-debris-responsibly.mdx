## H2: Understanding your Renovation Waste 
Understanding the types of waste your renovation will produce sets the stage for efficient and responsible disposal. Common types of renovation debris include wooden planks from removed structures, old appliances, cement residue, and leftover tiles. If generated in large quantities, these materials may require specialized disposal methods.
