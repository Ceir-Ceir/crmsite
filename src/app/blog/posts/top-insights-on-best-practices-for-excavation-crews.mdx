## H2: Minimizing Risk through Engineering Controls

A common method to reduce the risk of ground collapse during an excavation is by benching, battering or shoring the sides of the site, as suggested by WorkSafe. At CRM, we incorporate these safety standards across all excavation tasks to prevent accidents and ensure the smooth progression of your project.
